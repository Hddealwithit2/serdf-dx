UNDERTALE BLUE: INTEGRITY

A lovingly detailed browser fangame concept where the blue SOUL of Integrity falls into the Underground long before Frisk, crossing paths with a watchful Flowey, the Royal Guard prototype, and a path that values rhythm, honesty, and impossible mercy.

Boss Encounter: Flowey's “Helpful” Tutorial

Move the blue SOUL with Arrow Keys / WASD. In blue mode, gravity matters: jump to clear vine rings, land on platforms, and survive long enough to expose Flowey's fake friendliness. Build Mercy to unlock the Integrity route.

Story Pillars

Route Design

  • Integrity Route: Act honestly, protect NPCs, and spare Flowey after seeing through the lie.
  • Fracture Route: Exploit resets, betray allies, and let Flowey reshape battles.
  • Golden Echo: Hidden route nodding to Undertale Yellow with dual-SOUL challenge rooms.
Ruins: Toriel teaches blue-mode jumps over collapsed tile bridges.
Snowdin Outskirts: Integrity learns patterned parries from wandering sentries.
Waterfall: Truth trials compare what the player says with what they actually do.
Core Prototype: Flowey hijacks save points and narratively “edits” rooms in real time.

Why it feels like Undertale

This prototype leans on concise dialogue, readable bullet patterns, reactive narration, and route-sensitive themes. Integrity's blue SOUL physics are inspired by Papyrus-style gravity attacks, while the cast focus and hidden route structure nod toward the style of Undertale Yellow. Flowey remains central: charming, omnipresent, and terrifying once the mask slips.
