What Changed

Presentation

This revision strips away the glossy web-app feel and aims for a much more Undertale-like interface: stark black backgrounds, heavy white frames, bright menu colors, terse dialogue, and a battle box that is the clear center of attention.

Integrity Route Hook

Integrity plays differently from Frisk or Clover. The SOUL is blue from the start, so gravity is always active. Jumps must be deliberate, which makes Flowey's rings and pellet drops feel unfair in exactly the way he wants.

Act Options

  • Observe exposes the fake smile.
  • Tell Truth raises Mercy faster.
  • Mercy only works once Flowey loses control.
  • Fight provokes nastier attacks.

Undertale Yellow Influence

The tone takes notes from how Undertale Yellow expands the setting while still respecting the original battle language: clear motifs, route-sensitive dialogue, and one strong gameplay identity for the human SOUL.

Controls: move with ← → or A D, jump with ↑, W, or Space. Survive the attack waves, then use ACT and MERCY to break Flowey's script.
